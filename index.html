🇵🇦 Panamanian Culture

A culture full of history, diversity, and traditions

Panama is a country that is located in Central America.

It is a multicultural country which has Indigenous, African, European, Caribbean, and Asian influences.

Panamanian culture is a mixture of traditions that have been passed from one generation to another.

Panamanians are known for their hospitality and their strong connection to traditions.

🍲 Traditional Food

Flavors that represent Panama

Traditional Cuisine

Panamanian cuisine includes many dishes that combine local ingredients and cultural influences.

Sancocho

Sancocho is a traditional soup that is usually prepared with chicken, vegetables, herbs, and other ingredients.

Rice and Beans

Rice and beans are popular foods which are commonly served with meat, chicken, or fish.

💃 Traditional Dances & Clothing

Traditions that express Panama's cultural identity

The Pollera

The Pollera is Panama's traditional dress which is famous for its colorful embroidery and elegant design.

Tamborito

Tamborito is a traditional dance that combines music, singing, drums, and movement.

Folk Music

Panamanian folk music includes rhythms that reflect African, Indigenous, and Spanish influences.

🎉 Festivals & Traditions

Celebrations that bring communities together

Panamanian Carnival

Carnival is a famous celebration that includes music, costumes, dancing, parades, and traditional characters.

National Celebrations

November is a special month when Panamanians celebrate important national events.

Family Traditions

Panamanian families have traditions that help preserve the country's cultural identity.

🌴 Places & Heritage

Places where history, nature, and culture meet

Panama Canal

The Panama Canal is an engineering project that connects the Atlantic and Pacific Oceans.

San Blas Islands

San Blas is a beautiful region where visitors can enjoy Caribbean beaches and learn about Guna culture.

Indigenous Heritage

Panama has Indigenous communities whose traditions, art, languages, and crafts are an important part of the country's heritage.
